# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to the maintainers — the EY Cyber AI Centre of Excellence
platform team — through the organisation's internal security-reporting channel, or by a private
security advisory on this repository. Please do not open a public issue.

Include what you did, what happened, what you expected, and the `request_id` of an affected request
if you have one. You will get an acknowledgement, an assessment, and notice when a fix is released.

## Supported versions

Security fixes are made on the latest release.

The threat model, the protections in place and the known limitations are in
